Mental Training for Better Performance

Whether you compete in sports or perform in ballet, dance or on stage, performing at your best involves more than physical preparation and technique. Learning to manage your mental state is important, too. At Brave Athletes Club, we introduce practical skills for focus, emotional regulation and pre-performance routines that can be used in both everyday practice and important performances.

What We Work On

Before a Performance

  • Managing your mindset before competitions and performances
  • Using breathing to find a calm, focused state
  • Building a helpful pre-performance routine
  • Directing attention to what matters right now

During a Performance

  • Resetting after mistakes
  • Maintaining focus
  • Using constructive self-talk and simple cues
  • Regaining composure during a game or performance

After a Performance

  • Reflecting on your experience
  • Recognizing what went well
  • Setting your next goals
  • Turning insights into everyday habits

Practical Activities

  • Attention and focus exercises
  • Practicing performance routines
  • Visualization and mental rehearsal
  • Discussion and feedback
  • Simple reflection and progress notes

Who Is This For?

  • People who feel nervous before competitions or performances
  • Those who find it difficult to stay focused
  • Those who struggle to recover mentally after mistakes
  • Anyone who wants to bring their practice into performance
  • Those who want to develop mental skills alongside technical and expressive abilities

Our Approach at Brave Athletes Club

We believe mental skills, like physical and technical skills, can be developed through regular practice. The goal is not to eliminate every nervous feeling, but to learn useful ways to prepare, refocus and respond to different situations.

Our approach emphasizes clear, practical strategies that can become part of everyday training, supporting each person's development in sports and the performing arts.
