Autumn Event 2026

Adult Ball Game
Event

Run, laugh, and enjoy playing seriously for two hours. This is an autumn event for adults to enjoy movement, connection, and the simple joy of playing.

Saturday, November 7

Enjoy moving, laughing, and playing again.

As adults, we often have fewer chances to move freely and play with full energy. This event is designed for adults to enjoy physical activity in a relaxed and friendly atmosphere.

The goal is not only to win. We value safe movement, laughter, communication, and the feeling of enjoying sport together. People who have not exercised recently are also welcome to join at their own pace.

About Brave Athletes Club Events

Brave Athletes Club values not only training and performance, but also the joy of movement and human connection. Through seasonal events, we create opportunities for children and adults to move, laugh, learn, and connect with others.
