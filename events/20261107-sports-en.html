Autumn Event 2026

Autumn Sports
Festival

A fun sports event for everyone, from young children to adults. Enjoy ball games, recreational activities, and playful challenges together.

Saturday, November 7

Move, play, and enjoy together.

This event is designed so that children, adults, families, and individuals can all enjoy moving together. The focus is not only on competition, but also on having fun, trying different movements, and sharing the experience with others.

Activities include running, dodging, balancing, reacting, and cooperating with teammates. Participants can join at their own pace, regardless of age or sports experience.

For Everyone

Young children and adults can enjoy the same event in ways that suit their age and ability. You do not need sports experience to take part.

Family Participation Welcome

Families, friends, and individual participants are all welcome. We hope this event becomes a fun opportunity for people of different generations to move and connect together.

What We Value

Brave Athletes Club believes that enjoying movement is an important part of developing physical confidence and a positive relationship with sport. Through playful activities, participants can naturally experience running, reacting, balancing, cooperating, and trying new movements.

Our goal is to create a welcoming event where everyone can enjoy being active at their own pace.
