Christmas Event 2026

Christmas Bowling
Tournament

A fun Christmas event for children, adults, and seniors. Enjoy bowling, friendly competition, and warm community connection at the end of the year.

Saturday, December 19

A year-end bowling event everyone can enjoy.

Bowling is an activity that many people can enjoy together, regardless of age or athletic experience. This Christmas tournament is designed to be a cheerful event where families, friends, children, adults, and seniors can all take part.

The goal is not only to compete, but also to enjoy movement, connection, and a fun seasonal atmosphere with the Brave Athletes Club community.
