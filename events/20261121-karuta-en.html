Autumn Event 2026

Casual Karuta
Event

A friendly autumn event where children and adults can enjoy Hyakunin Isshu, disaster-prevention karuta, and sports karuta together. Enjoy learning, movement, focus, and communication through play.

Saturday, November 21

Learn and connect through playful karuta activities.

This casual karuta event is designed so that children, adults, and families can join comfortably, even if they are not familiar with competitive karuta.

Through Hyakunin Isshu, disaster-prevention karuta, and sports karuta, participants can enjoy concentration, quick reactions, communication, and learning in a relaxed atmosphere. The focus is not on playing perfectly, but on enjoying the experience together.

What We Value

  • Introducing disaster-prevention learning and movement skills in a fun way

Recommended For

About Brave Athletes Club Events

Brave Athletes Club values not only physical training, but also learning, communication, and warm connections between people. Our seasonal events are designed to help participants enjoy movement, play, and interaction in a safe and welcoming environment.
